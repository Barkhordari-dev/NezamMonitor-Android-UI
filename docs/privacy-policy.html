Privacy Policy — NezamMonitor Pro

Effective date: 22 November 2026
App: NezamMonitor Pro (package: com.nezammonitor.app), version 3.11.8
Developer: Barkhordari-dev
Contact: mmahdib2020@gmail.com

NezamMonitor Pro is a case-management app for engineers of the Yazd Engineering Organization who use the Yazd system at http://service.yazdnezam.ir:8033/panel/. It helps engineers manage their engineering cases, fees, visit reports, receipts and follow-ups on their phone, in sync with the Windows version.

1. Data we collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, sell, or share any personal data. The developer has no server that receives your data and uses no analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or third-party tracker in the app.

2. Data stored on your device

All case data — cases, fees, reports, receipts and follow-ups that you enter or import — is stored locally on your own device. The built-in directory of Yazd offices and engineers is bundled with the app. Word reports are generated locally on your device.

3. Data you choose to exchange

  • Yazd system updates: only when you choose to update from the Yazd system, the app contacts that system (service.yazdnezam.ir:8033/panel) to fetch your case data. That data is shown and stored on your device.
  • .nzmdata files: you can import or export case packages (.nzmdata) to exchange data with the Windows version. Importing and exporting happen only when you choose to do so, and the files go where you choose to save or share them.

4. Permissions

The app requests only these permissions:

  • Internet and network state — to fetch updates from the Yazd system when you ask for them.
  • Storage — to read .nzmdata files you import and to save the Word reports and exports you create.

The app does not request access to SMS, calls, location, contacts, microphone, or camera, and it shows no advertisements.

5. Data sharing

We do not share your data with anyone. Your case data never leaves your device except through the two actions above, both initiated by you (updating from the Yazd system, or importing/exporting a file you choose).

6. Data retention and deletion

Your data stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app removes the app's locally stored data from your device. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; if you have exported files, delete those files yourself.

7. Children's privacy

The app is a professional tool for engineers and is not directed at children.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new effective date.
